# Delay character-sheet capture until name chooser closes

**Date:** 2026-10-02 12:00
**Files:** Assets/_project/Scripts/1-New Character/7-ChooseName/ChooseNamePresenter.cs
**Severity:** medium

## Problem

The final name-selection window was visible in the first generated character-sheet image.

## Root Cause

The presenter invoked the screenshot workflow before it started the one-second hide animation.
`TakeScreenshot` captures its first frame after 0.1 seconds, so it could capture the still-visible chooser.

## Solution

The completion of the hide animation now destroys the chooser and only then invokes the screenshot workflow.

## Prevention

When a transition starts a screen capture, invoke the capture callback from the completion of any closing animation rather than in parallel with it.
